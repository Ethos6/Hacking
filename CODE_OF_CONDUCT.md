# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people at every level, from first-time learners to experienced professionals.

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Keep discussions about the content of the project.
- Don't share illegal content, stolen data, credentials, or instructions for harming specific people or organizations.
- Harassment, personal attacks and spam lead to removal from the project.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
